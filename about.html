About Hari Travels

Safe journeys across India since 2015

Hari Travels started in Chennai with one bus and a simple idea: safe, comfortable and affordable bus travel for everyone. Today we run daily services to Bangalore, Coimbatore, Madurai, Hyderabad and Pollachi with a modern fleet of AC sleeper, seater and luxury coaches.

All our buses have GPS tracking, experienced drivers and safety equipment. Our team works day and night so you can book tickets online in a few easy steps and travel stress free.

Our mission is simple: best price, best buses and happy passengers on every trip.
